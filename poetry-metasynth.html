Synthesis parameters

How the mapping works

Hue → lexicon / theme (12 word-families around the color wheel)
Horizontal position → reading order (time)
Vertical position → register: top = airy/light diction, bottom = grounded/heavy diction
Brightness → tone: luminous vs. shadowed modifiers
Saturation → vividness: likelihood of imagery-dense phrasing
Contrast between neighbors → punctuation and line breaks
