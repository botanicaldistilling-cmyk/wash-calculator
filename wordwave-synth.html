How the mapping works

Word length → pitch: short words ring high, long words sit low
Letter sum (hash) → timbre family / color, same 12-bucket wheel as VerseSynth
Vowel density → filter brightness: open vowels sound brighter, consonants darker
Line position → register drift across the poem (top brighter, bottom lower)
Word position in line → stereo pan, left→right as it's read
Punctuation → rhythm: commas breathe, periods rest, "!" accents, "?" bends pitch up
Capitalized words → a short percussive accent
Rhyming line-endings → echo / delay
